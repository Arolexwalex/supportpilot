Resetting Your Password and Account Security

If you've forgotten your password, go to the login page and click "Forgot password?" You'll be asked to enter the email address associated with your account. Within a few minutes, you'll receive an email containing a reset link. Click the reset link, choose a new password, and you'll be logged in automatically once it's confirmed.

The reset link expires after 60 minutes for security reasons. If it expires before you use it, simply repeat the process to receive a new one.

We recommend choosing a password that is at least 12 characters long and includes a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid reusing passwords from other services, since a breach elsewhere could put your Nimbus account at risk too.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available for all accounts and strongly recommended for anyone storing sensitive files. You can enable it under Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication, using either an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator or Authy) or SMS text codes.

If you suspect your account has been compromised — for example, you notice files or shares you didn't create — change your password immediately, revoke all active sessions under Account Settings > Security > Active Sessions, and contact support so we can review your account's recent activity log with you.

Account lockout: after 5 failed login attempts within 15 minutes, your account will be temporarily locked for 30 minutes as a security measure. This is automatic and cannot be bypassed by support for security reasons, but it will unlock itself after the cooldown period.

We will never ask for your password via email, chat, or phone. Any message claiming to be from Nimbus support asking for your password directly is not from us — please report it to security@nimbus.example.
