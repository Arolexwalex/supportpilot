Nimbus Support Hours and Contact Information

Our support team is available 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, Eastern Time. During these hours, you can expect a response to live chat within 5 minutes and a response to email within 2 hours.

Outside of business hours, you can still submit a support ticket through the Help Center, and our team will respond as soon as we're back online, typically first thing the next business day.

For urgent issues affecting your ability to access your account or data, we offer emergency support via our status page at status.nimbus.example, which is monitored 24/7 by our on-call engineering team, separate from general customer support.

You can reach us through three channels: live chat (available directly inside the Nimbus app, bottom-right corner), email at support@nimbus.example, and our community forum at community.nimbus.example, where other users and our team answer common questions publicly.

Enterprise plan customers have access to a dedicated account manager and a private Slack channel for faster response times, with a guaranteed response within 30 minutes during business hours as part of their service level agreement.

We do not currently offer phone support, as we've found that chat and email allow our team to include screenshots, links, and detailed technical steps that are hard to communicate over a call. If your issue is complex, a support agent may offer to schedule a video call to walk through it together.

Holidays observed: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Support is unavailable on these dates, and tickets submitted will be answered the next business day.
