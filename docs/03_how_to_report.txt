How to Report a Suspected Scam in Nigeria

If you believe you have encountered or fallen victim to a scam, you can report it through the following official channels:

Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC): Nigeria's principal anti-fraud agency investigates financial crimes including Ponzi schemes, advance fee fraud, and cybercrime. Reports can be made through the EFCC's official website or by visiting the nearest EFCC office.

Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC): Handles complaints related to telecom-based fraud, including SMS phishing and fraudulent calls impersonating telecom or financial institutions.

Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and NIBSS: For fraud involving bank transfers or digital payments, report immediately to your bank's fraud desk, which can sometimes freeze a transaction if reported quickly, in addition to notifying the CBN.

Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC): The appropriate body for reporting suspected fraudulent investment schemes and unregistered investment platforms.

General guidance: act quickly, since faster reporting improves the chance of recovering funds or preventing further loss. Keep screenshots of all messages, payment records, and any contact information from the suspected scammer, as these will be needed for a report.
