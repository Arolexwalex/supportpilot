Troubleshooting Common Issues

Files not syncing: first, check your internet connection. Next, confirm the Nimbus desktop app shows a green checkmark icon (synced) rather than a gray or red icon. If it's stuck on syncing, right-click the Nimbus icon in your system tray and select "Pause Sync," wait 10 seconds, then select "Resume Sync" — this resolves the vast majority of sync issues by resetting the connection.

Slow upload or download speeds: large files (over 1GB) can take time depending on your internet speed. If speeds seem unusually slow even for small files, try switching from Wi-Fi to a wired connection, or check whether a VPN is active, since some VPNs significantly slow down file transfer speeds.

"Storage full" error even though you have space available: this can happen if you have a large number of files in your Trash, since deleted files still count against your storage quota for 30 days before permanent deletion. Empty your Trash from the web app under Files > Trash > Empty Trash to reclaim space immediately.

Shared folder not appearing for a team member: confirm the invite email wasn't sent to a different email address than the one they're logged in with. Shared folders are tied to the exact email address invited, not the person generally — if in doubt, remove and re-share using the email they actually log in with.

Desktop app won't open or crashes on startup: try fully quitting the app (not just closing the window) via Task Manager (Windows) or Activity Monitor (Mac), then reopening it. If that doesn't help, uninstalling and reinstalling the latest version from nimbus.example/download resolves the large majority of startup crashes, since most are caused by a corrupted local cache from an interrupted update.

Two-factor authentication codes not working: make sure the time on your phone is set to automatic/network time, since authenticator apps generate codes based on precise timing, and a phone clock that's drifted out of sync will generate incorrect codes even though the app itself is working correctly.
