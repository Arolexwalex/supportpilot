Billing, Plans, and Subscriptions

Nimbus offers three plans: Free (2GB storage, single user), Pro ($9/month, 500GB storage, single user), and Team ($15/user/month, 2TB shared storage, unlimited team members).

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from Account Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately, and you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, so you keep your current plan's features until then.

Billing occurs monthly or annually, depending on what you selected at signup. Annual plans receive a 20% discount compared to paying monthly. You can switch between monthly and annual billing from the same Billing page.

If a payment fails, we'll retry it three times over 7 days and email you each time. If all three attempts fail, your account will be downgraded to the Free plan, though your files remain safe — you simply won't be able to upload new files beyond the Free plan's 2GB limit until you update your payment method.

Refunds: we offer a full refund within 14 days of any charge, no questions asked, for both new subscriptions and renewals. After 14 days, we do not offer partial refunds for unused time in a billing period, but you're welcome to cancel at any time to stop future charges.

To cancel your subscription entirely, go to Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your account will remain active with full features until the end of your current billing period, after which it converts to the Free plan automatically — your data is not deleted.

Invoices and receipts are available under Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History, and are also emailed automatically at the time of each charge. For businesses needing a specific invoice format for accounting purposes, contact billing@nimbus.example.
