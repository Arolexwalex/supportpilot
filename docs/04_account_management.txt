Managing Your Nimbus Account

To update your email address, go to Account Settings > Profile > Email Address. You'll need to confirm the change by clicking a verification link sent to your new email before it takes effect, and your old email will also receive a notification of the change as a security measure.

Team accounts can add or remove members from Account Settings > Team > Manage Members. Only account owners and admins can add or remove members. When a member is removed, their personal files remain in the shared workspace, but their individual account and login access are revoked immediately.

To permanently delete your account, go to Account Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action is irreversible after a 30-day grace period, during which you can log back in to cancel the deletion. After 30 days, all files, folders, and account data are permanently erased from our systems and cannot be recovered.

Exporting your data: before deleting your account, or simply to keep a backup, you can export all your files as a single zip archive from Account Settings > Data > Export My Data. This process can take up to 24 hours for large accounts, and you'll receive an email with a download link once it's ready.

Changing your account's primary owner (Team plans only): the current owner can transfer ownership to another team member from Account Settings > Team > Transfer Ownership. The new owner will then have full billing and administrative control, and the previous owner is automatically demoted to a regular admin role.

Merging two accounts is not currently supported. If you've accidentally created two separate accounts, contact support and we can help you manually move files from one account to another, though this is a manual process and may take a few business days depending on the amount of data involved.
